FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is the checklist really free?

Yes. There is no payment required to receive it.

What rating is this designed for?

The primary audience is intermediate players roughly from 800–1600 who want a clearer path to their next level of improvement.

Do I need to know chess notation?

No. The checklist is designed to be practical and accessible.

Does this teach openings?

Not as its main purpose. The goal is to help you determine what you should actually be working on before spending large amounts of time on a specific area such as opening theory.

Will this increase my rating?

There are no guaranteed rating gains. The checklist is designed to give you a clearer process for identifying weaknesses and choosing training priorities. Your results depend on how you apply it.

How is this different from watching YouTube or asking for chess advice online?

There is no shortage of chess advice. The challenge is determining which advice applies to your games and current weaknesses. The checklist gives you a structured process for evaluating your own improvement needs instead of simply giving you more content to consume.

Do I need to buy anything?

No. The checklist is free. Future Chess Compass products and coaching may be available separately.

What happens to my email?

You'll receive the checklist and occasional Chess Compass emails about practical chess improvement. You can unsubscribe at any time.
